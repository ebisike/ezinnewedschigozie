02 — Memories

Our Story

From a Maths lesson in 2015 to forever — this is our story.

January 2015

Where It All Began

It all began on the 18th day of January 2015, when we were introduced by a mutual friend, and I (Chigozie) was told that she (Ezinne) needed a tutor in Maths and Physics, so as a generous boy, I decided to teach her without any payments. I also observed on the first day we met that her eyes were quite spectacular, gentle and beautiful to behold. Although I didn't think much about any relationship at the time, my main focus was to impart my knowledge. From there, I took my time to teach her during night classes, and evening sessions. The aftermath of that engagement was that she excelled in those courses, giving her a solid foundation to go on and become one of the best in her class, and that made me happy. We became close friends from then, and we have both not looked back.

Along the Way

Our First Adventure

We have had various adventures from traveling to FECA National Convention in Benin to seeing a movie (Wedding Party) at a cinema in Owerri and visiting the beach in Lagos. They were all beautiful days and they formed our first set of adventures with many more to come.

The Journey

Through Everything

The journey to this point was not a smooth sail. It was one that tested our love and commitment for each other. There were moments that felt difficult and exciting at the same time. We also had days where we were over the moon over each other without caring about any other thing in the world and one thing I am grateful for is the fact that we had each other's back at all times. When I lost my dad, and felt lost, she was there all through. When she had an accident and went through severe pain, I was there for her. It is that mutual commitment for one another that continued to keep us going and that has grown much stronger.

19 · 12 · 2026

Forever Begins

This is a story that is only just getting started and I am grateful to God for making this a reality. So, as this journey begins, one thing I am sure of is that WE WILL ROCK THIS WORLD TOGETHER! Baby m, we are only just getting started, and I know what is ahead of us is far greater than anything that is behind us. Let the journey begin...
